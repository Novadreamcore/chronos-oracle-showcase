# What is CHRONOS ORACLE?

CHRONOS ORACLE is an experimental desktop research workbench for structured AI-assisted investigation.

A conventional chat system often compresses a question into one answer. CHRONOS instead tries to preserve multiple competing explanations long enough to compare them.

The design principle is simple:

> A plausible answer should survive criticism before it becomes a final answer.

The workbench therefore separates activities that are often mixed together:

- collecting the question and known constraints,
- generating competing hypotheses,
- checking evidence and provenance,
- searching for contradictions,
- attacking weak assumptions,
- revising failed candidates,
- synthesizing surviving material,
- auditing the final result.

CHRONOS does not guarantee truth. Its purpose is to make the research process more inspectable and to reduce the chance that a fluent first answer is mistaken for a verified conclusion.
