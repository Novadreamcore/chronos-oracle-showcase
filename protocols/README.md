# Public protocol excerpts

These files are **showcase excerpts**, not the complete internal protocols.

They are intentionally limited to:

- the research question or prompt class,
- the reason the benchmark exists,
- the type of controls or failure modes being examined,
- selected curated output trajectories where they are useful to understand the behavior.

They do not contain the full internal prompt stack, scoring system, gating thresholds or source code.
